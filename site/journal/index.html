How many months your cash lasts at today’s rate of spending, after the income you expect. Tarn measures it every morning from real balances.

The lowest balance you agree to keep. It covers a few weeks of fixed costs, money held for others such as sales tax, and one bad surprise.

How much more cash leaves than arrives in a typical month. A business can be profitable on paper and still burn cash while it waits to be paid.

A week-by-week view of cash for the next quarter, built from expected receipts and known payments. The standard short-range forecast lenders ask for.

The average number of days between sending an invoice and being paid. Every day it falls is cash you hold sooner.
