Read-only, by design.

Tarn can see your balances and transactions. It can never move money, and it never sees your bank login. Here is how the rest of your data is looked after.

Request security documents See how data flows

Where your data goes, and where it can’t.

Four stops, each with one job. Money only ever moves inside your bank, never through Tarn.

  1. 1

    Your bank

    You sign in on your bank’s own page. Your password stays there.

    11,000+ banks
  2. Consent you can revoke
  3. 2

    Regulated data provider

    Issues Tarn a read-only token. No payment permissions exist on it.

    Read-only token
  4. Balances and transactions, TLS
  5. 3

    Tarn

    Builds your forecast. Data is encrypted at rest, tokens kept in a separate vault.

    AES-256 at rest
  6. Signed-in sessions only
  7. 4

    You and your team

    Roles decide who sees balances, who edits, and who can connect accounts.

    4 people

No path back to your money. Tarn holds no payment permissions, so it cannot move, send or withdraw funds.
