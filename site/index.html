Tarn connects through a regulated bank-data provider with read-only access. We never see or store your bank login, and Tarn cannot move money. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can disconnect any account in one click.

It starts from your real balances and the bills, invoices and payroll already scheduled, then learns your regular income and spending from the last twelve months. Most teams see weekly balances land within a few percent once a month of actuals has been matched.

Tarn reads from the major cloud ledgers, payroll and billing tools, and over 11,000 banks and credit unions. If you keep part of the picture in a spreadsheet, you can import it or link a sheet that updates on its own.
